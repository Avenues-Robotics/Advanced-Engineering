### **2. Concept of Operations (ConOps)**
1. * Normal teleop operation*
	The robot begins each teleop cycle by collecting three artifacts from the field floor using the intake subsystem. The artifact is transferred internally to the launcher. The driver positions the robot at the preferred firing zone and fires. The artifacts enter the classifier for 3 x 3 = 9 points. The robot immediately returns to collect three more artifacts. Target cycle time is under 5 seconds. This repeats for the full 2-minute teleop period.
2. *Close autonomous period*
	The robot begins with 3 pre-loaded artifacts. Without driver input, it fires all 3 artifacts into the classifier from the close zone.  The robot picks up additional groups of 3 balls from the spike marks and fires all 3 into the classifier.  This is repeated for all three spike marks.
3. *Far autonomous period*
	The robot begins with 3 pre-loaded artifacts. Without driver input, it fires all 3 artifacts into the classifier from the far zone.  The robot picks up additional groups of 3 balls from the human player zone and moves back to the far one to fire three more balls.  The robot then repeats gathering balls that were released from the gate by the alliance partner robot and shoots them from the far zone.
4. *Edge case, all four robots can only shoot from the close zone*
	The robot navigates a tight scoring zone competing for space and dealing with collisions.
### **3. System-Level Requirements**
- The robot <span underline="true">shall</span> score artifacts into the classifier during the autonomous period.
- The robot <span underline="true">shall</span> score a minimum of 3 artifacts into the classifier per teleop cycle.
- The robot <span underline="true">shall</span> leave the starting zone during the autonomous period.
- The robot <span underline="true">should</span> achieve the pattern bonus during autonomous.
- The robot <span underline="true">should</span> clear the gate during teleop to enable continued scoring.
- The robot <span underline="true">should</span> support itself in the base zone such that another robot can fully park to achieve the endgame support bonus.
*Key Driving Requirement (KDR) candidates: *
	- *Rapid classifier scoring (teleop): 3 pts per artifact, highest-volume scoring action. *
	- *Endgame base support: 20 pts guaranteed if achieved, high return for single action.*
