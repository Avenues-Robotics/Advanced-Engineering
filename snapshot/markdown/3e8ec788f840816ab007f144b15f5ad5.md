You just made a big programming mistake!!! It's ok, we made you do it...
Notice that your AutoPushbotLeft and AutoPushbotRight OpModes both use copies of the same drive() and rotate() methods. What if you decided you wanted to change something in the drive method? You would have to fix both AutoPushbotLeft and AutoPushbotRight. What if you decided you needed to change something after creating 10 more op modes using the same code!!! To avoid this scenario, programmers try to make their code modular by creating separate classes for commonly used functions.
In this assignment, you are going to create a Drive class that contains both your drive() and rotate() methods. You will then import your Drive class into your OpModes which will make your OpModes much simpler and easier to follow.
This is a little more complex than what you have done in the past, since we are dealing with two different class files that have to communicate with each other. This video will guide you through the process of creating and using your class.
