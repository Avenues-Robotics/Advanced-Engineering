In Robotics we rarely model just one part.  Those parts have to fit together in an "**assembly**." Our most complex assembly is the entire robot, but that robot is usually made of many separate smaller assemblies.  These assemblies are put together with a feature OnShape calls a **"mate connector." **In this tutorial you will get started with mate connectors by combining the three parts of the hinged box shown below into an assembly.
![](media/da97c94644cb6a93.png)
For this tutorial, so you can focus on learning how to mate parts <br>together, you will use a starter file that has the top and bottom of the<br> box already modeled.
