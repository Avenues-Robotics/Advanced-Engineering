This set of problems is designed to teach you the physics of a flywheel-based shooter.  You will learn about:
- friction forces
- spring forces and energy stored in a spring
- the forces on an object as it slips and slides
- rotational motion
- the forces on a round objects as it rolls and slips
This set is challenging since it packages a lot of first year physics into just one set.  Use the linked readings to review unfamiliar concepts and help you fully understand the systems you are modeling.
