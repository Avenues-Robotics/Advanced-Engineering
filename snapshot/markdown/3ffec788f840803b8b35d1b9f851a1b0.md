3D printed parts are a great way to customize your robot.  You have infinite control over the exact size and shape of the part, but you still need to mount it to existing robot parts.  This tutorial shows you how to add perfectly placed screw holes, counter bore screw holes, and heat set inserts to a 3D printed part.
