# **FTC DECODE 2025–2026**
## **Launcher Control Software Requirements**
### **Flowdown**
This document covers the control software's part of the launcher, not the mechanism which belongs to the mechanical requirements document by Jonathan.
- The robot shall score artifacts into the classifier during the autonomous period.
- The robot shall score a minimum of 3 artifacts into the classifier per teleop cycle.
### **Operating Scenarios (ConOps)**
1. Teleop firing cycle
	The driver positions the robot and issues a fire command. The control software must already know whether an artifact is staged and whether the flywheel is up to speed, and must handle the fire and reset within the roughly 5 second target cycle time.
2. Autonomous firing sequence
	At match start, the robot fires 3 preloaded artifacts without driver input, then repeats this as additional artifacts are collected. The control software must decide on its own when to fire, timed with the autonomous movement, with no human pressing a button.
### **Functional requirements**
1. The control software shall check the staging sensor and know within 50 ms whether an artifact has arrived.
2. The control software shall turn the flywheel motor on and bring it up to shooting speed for the current distance (close or far) before a shot is allowed.
3. The control software shall put the launcher back in a ready state after a shot, without the driver having to reset anything.
4. The control software shall not fire while the flywheel is still below shooting speed, and shall fire automatically as soon as it reaches speed.
5. During autonomous, the control software shall fire on its own, timed to when the robot has driven into position, with no driver pressing a button.
### **Performance requirements**
**Shall Requirements**
1. Loop speed: The control software <span underline="true">shall</span> check its sensors and update its decisions at least every 20 milliseconds.
2. Spin-up time: The flywheel <span underline="true">shall</span> reach shooting speed within 500 ms of being turned on, so a shot doesn't come out too slow.
3. Reset time: The launcher <span underline="true">shall</span> be back in a ready state within 100 ms of a shot being confirmed fired.
4. Sensor accuracy: The staging-sensor check <span underline="true">shall</span> correctly tell whether an artifact is present in at least 29 of 30 tries.
### **Interface requirements**
1. The control software <span underline="true">shall</span> check the staging sensor at least every 20 milliseconds, and shall only trust a reading once it sees the same result three times in a row, not just once. This keeps one noisy reading from being treated as true.
2. The control software <span underline="true">shall</span> send a power value between -1 and 1 to the flywheel motor at least every 20 milliseconds.
3. The control software <span underline="true">shall</span> send the launcher's current state and measured speed to the driver's screen at least every 20 milliseconds, so the driver can see what the launcher is doing.
4. The control software <span underline="true">shall</span> receive fire commands from the driver's controller.
5. The control software <span underline="true">shall</span> receive a signal from the Transfer subsystem saying an artifact is staged, and that signal shall be based on the Transfer subsystem's own sensor reading, not just a flag either program sets on its own. *(Confirmed with Transfer subsystem owner.)*
### **Should requirements**
1. The control software <span underline="true">should</span> keep the flywheel's shooting-speed settings in one clearly labeled place in the code, so they can be changed quickly without hunting through the whole program.
2. The control software <span underline="true">should</span> keep a log file of the flywheel's actual speed over time, to help figure out why a shot was missed after the match.
### **Key Driving Requirements**
1. Sensor accuracy
	Highest risk to reliable scoring. A missed or false reading on the staging sensor, especially while the robot is getting bumped by another one, can make a shot fire too early, too late, or not at all.
2. Spin-up time
	Hardest requirement to verify early. Flywheel spin-up time depends on battery charge, and match day batteries are always variable.
