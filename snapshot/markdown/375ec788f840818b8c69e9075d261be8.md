Let's get started with a quick OnShape CAD tutorial.
CAD (computer-aided design) us used to efficiently create and assemble 3D models of engineering designs.  Creating accurate 3D models digitally allows designers to visualize their creations, test ideas, and communicate their ideas with teammates or customers.  There are two major CAD skills that robotics students should master: (1) part creation and (2) part assembly.  These first few assignments will teach you how to use Onshape for part creation, then we will move onto part assembly.
After you sign in, **Create** a new document and get started.  Follow along with the instructor (Too Tall Toby) on this video to create a simple 3D part: [https://youtu.be/mdauyI8Dc_0?si=KVMQO5YZn44wB5A3&t=132](https://youtu.be/mdauyI8Dc_0?si=KVMQO5YZn44wB5A3&t=132)
