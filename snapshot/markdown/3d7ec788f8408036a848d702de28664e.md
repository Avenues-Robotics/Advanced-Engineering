### Overview
This is the reflection you'll submit every two weeks, due the day before our one-on-one meeting. It's short by design: a quick, honest check on what you got done, what you learned, and where you're headed next. Keeping up with these will also make your End-of-Term and End-of-year reflections much easier to write, since they build directly on what you record here.
### Before You Start
- Create a new tab in your Advanced Robotics Reflections doc.  Name it “Bi-weekly Reflection - DATE”
- Look back through your Engineering Notebook entries from the past two weeks before you start writing. This should take a few minutes, not be something you're doing from memory.
- Pick out two or three entries that actually matter, a decision you made, a test result, a problem you ran into, and plan to cite them specifically rather than summarizing the whole two weeks in general terms.
- This isn't meant to take long. Twenty to thirty focused minutes is normal once your notebook is in good shape.
- Respond to every question, but for time’s sake, you don’t have to do *deep* reflection for every prompt.  Think about staying at the Report and Respond level for each question, and then dive deeper into Relate, Reason, and Reconstruct for one question per part.
### Part 1: What You Did
1. What did you work on over the past two weeks? Reference specific Engineering Notebook entries (by date) for what you did, what changed, and what prompted the change.
2. What's one thing you tried that didn't work, or a problem you're still stuck on? What have you already tried?
### Part 2: What You Learned
1. What's something you learned or figured out in the past two weeks, technical or otherwise?
2. Looking at what happened, why do you think it happened that way? Is there a pattern connecting this to something earlier in the year?
3. How does what you did over the past two weeks connect to your bigger goals, whether that's your project's goals or the goals you set for yourself in your Initial Reflection?
### Part 3: Looking Ahead
1. Based on what you learned, does your plan need to change? Is there a task you should drop, add, simplify, or approach differently going forward?
2. What are your goals for the next two weeks? Be specific enough that we can check in on them at our next meeting.
3. What support do you need from me, a teammate, or anyone else to hit those goals?
