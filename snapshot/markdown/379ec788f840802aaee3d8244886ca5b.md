# **FTC DECODE 2025–2026**
## **Shooter Subsystem Requirements**
### **Flowdown**
The following system-level requirements are owned by the shooter subsystem:
- The robot shall score artifacts into the classifier during the autonomous period.
- The robot shall score artifacts into the classifier during teleop.
- The robot shall score a minimum of 3 artifacts into the classifier per teleop cycle.
### **Operating Scenarios (ConOps)**
1. Close Shooting
	The shooter rapidly shoots three artifacts from the close zone and is then ready to receive more artifacts within 1 second.  There may be contact between robots from any direction; the robot shoots accurately while being pushed.
2. Far Shooting
	The shooter rapidly shoots three artifacts from the far zone and is then ready to receive more artifacts within 1 second.  There may be contact between robots from the side; the robot shoots accurately while being pushed sideways.
### **Functional requirements**
1. The shooter shall propel an artifact from the robot into the classifier opening.
2. The shooter shall receive an artifact from the intake subsystem and hold it in a ready-to-fire position.
3. The shooter shall return to a ready-to-receive state after each firing cycle without driver intervention.
### **Performance requirements**
**Shall Requirements**
1. Consistency: The shooter <span underline="true">shall</span> classify a minimum of 3 consecutive artifacts without a miss.
2. Throughput: The shooter <span underline="true">shall</span> complete a firing cycle in 2 seconds or less from ready-to-fire position.
3. Reliability: The shooter <span underline="true">shall</span> operate for the full 2.5-minute match duration without mechanical failure.
### **Interface requirements**
1. The shooter shall receive an artifact from the intake subsystem within ±10 mm of the shooter's intake point. *(Confirmed with intake subsystem owner.)*
2. The shooter shall signal ready-to-receive to the intake subsystem within 0.5 seconds of completing a firing cycle. *(Confirmed with intake subsystem owner.)*
### **Should requirements**
1. The shooter <span underline="true">should</span> clear a jammed artifact and return to a ready-to-fire state within 10 seconds of jam detection, with or without driver input.
2. The shooter <span underline="true">should</span> support independent adjustment of launch angle and/or launch speed by the control system, with a launch angle range of at least X degrees and a motor speed range of at least Y to Z RPM.  X, Y, and Z to be determined in initial testing.
