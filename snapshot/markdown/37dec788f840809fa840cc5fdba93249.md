In FTC Robotics we use a lot of manufactured parts in our robot designs.  Luckily, most of these parts have OnShape models already made, so we don't have to recreate every channel, bearing, and screw.  These parts are available in the FTC Parts Library that is connected to your OnShape account.
In this lesson by Caden Kim, you will use the FTC Parts Library to build the mechanism below.
![](media/42995c4a5ff72f50.png)
Sign in to OnShape and make sure you are in the Avenues Robotics organization, then create a new document.  Follow along with the video <br>to build the assembly.
**One more thing before you submit, In the real world, that wheel would not be very <br>stable in the center of the shaft.  Find a GoBilda component or two that will hold the wheel securely in place (note that there are tons of options).  Add that/those parts to your assembly.**
