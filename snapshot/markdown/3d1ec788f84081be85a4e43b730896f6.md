## Rubric
### Documentation
<columns>
	<column ratio="50">
		**Proficient**
		- Reflections reports and respond with specificity and detail: a concrete account of what happened (dates, numbers, events) and an honest response to it, what you thought, felt, or noticed, not a vague summary of either.
		- Check-ins and reflections reference Engineering Notebook entries recording what was done, what changed, and what prompted it (a design decision, a test result, an observation, a measurement, or a peer suggestion), and what comes next.
		- Reflection on each major project: what went well, what didn't, and what you would do differently.
	</column>
	<column ratio="50">
		**Advanced**
		In addition to Proficient…
		- Reflections relate, reason, and reconstruct: connect what happened to past experience or knowledge, reason about why it mattered or what caused it, and reconstructs your thinking or approach going forward.
		- Check-ins and reflections reference Engineering Notebook entries that include not just what changed and why, but which alternative fixes were considered and why the chosen approach was selected over others, connected to a specific requirement, MOP, or ConOps scenario where applicable.
		- When a design is modified in response to a test finding, an explicit diagnosis of whether the finding indicated a design problem, a build quality problem, or a requirements problem, recorded before the fix is described.
		- Documentation of project work is maintained in a shared digital project management tool (e.g. GitHub Projects) so all team members have visibility into the current state and history of each subsystem.
		- Project reflections that go beyond process, including an honest assessment of the quality of the engineering decisions, not just what happened, but whether they were good choices and why or why not.
		- Personal learning reflections that identify specific skills or concepts you are actively working to improve, with progress on those goals tracked across entries as a narrative of growth rather than isolated observations.
	</column>
</columns>
