## Rubric
### Mechanics
<columns>
	<column ratio="50">
		**Proficient**
		- At least one physics analysis directly tied to a design decision with work shown in units and assumptions stated explicitly.
			- Examples: required motor torque to lift or move a known load, gear ratio selection, structural member sizing; joint reach to satisfy a height or extension requirement, range of motion analysis.
		- Concepts at this level: force (N), torque (N·m), mass (kg), weight (N), distance / displacement (m), angle (degrees or radians), gear ratio (unitless), mechanical advantage (unitless), velocity (m/s), and rotational speed (RPM or rad/s).
	</column>
	<column ratio="50">
		**Advanced**
		In addition to Proficient…
		- A connected chain of physics analysis covering the primary load path or control loop for a subsystem, not just one calculation, but a sequence where the output of one step is the input to the next.
			- Examples: motor torque to gear reduction to output force to factor of safety on structure; input motion to linkage geometry to end effector position and velocity.
		- A factor of safety calculated for at least one critical element: the worst-case load identified, the rated capacity of the material or component determined, the ratio computed, and an explicit judgment of whether the margin is acceptable given the consequence of failure.
		- Concepts at this level (builds on Level 1): acceleration (m/s²), angular acceleration (rad/s²), moment of inertia (kg·m²), normal force (N), friction force (N), coefficient of friction (unitless), tension (N), compression (N), shear force (N), bending moment (N·m), stress (Pa), factor of safety (unitless), rated load / capacity (N or N·m), work (J), and power (W).
	</column>
</columns>
